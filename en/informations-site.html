Privacy and audience

Aggregate viewing statistics are collected with Umami, without audience measurement or advertising cookies. They show which sections are viewed, which languages are used and how many CVs are downloaded.

Accessibility preferences and reading progress remain stored in your browser. They are not sent to the statistics service.

About Umami ↗

The public counter presents daily estimates of the browsers, cities and countries represented, separate from the Umami statistics. CountAPI provides the counters. ipwho.is estimates the city and country from the IP address, without GPS. Only hashed location codes and aggregate counters are sent to CountAPI; geographic labels are not published. The browser remembers its participation to avoid repeated increments. Locations have been recorded since October 22, 2026, without reconstructing earlier visits. These requests respect the refusal of statistics and do-not-track settings. If a source is unavailable, a dash replaces the figure.
